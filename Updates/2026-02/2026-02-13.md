Took the MARTA to Sandy Springs. Leaving the hotel I ditched everything but the pack (threw out some useless bags I had too), so what I have now is what I will have forever.

Walked around Sandy Springs for a bit. Went to the REI to get fuel since I couldn't fly with it (now what I have is what I will have forever). REI lady wished me luck and gave me a sticker.

Ubered from Sandy Springs to Amicalola which I was surprised worked. Uber driver was super chill about it tho and said he was wanting to hike up the falls anyways, so I felt less guilty about making him drive 90 minutes. He clocked me as a prospective thru hiker and gave me a bunch of advice and stories and then dropped me off in the parking lot at the base of the falls where I thought the lodge was. The lodge was not at the base of the falls.
