Tomorrow we are taking an arguably dumb gamble. The kennebec river has to be crossed via a ferry that runs until 2PM. If we can burn thru 18 (easyish) miles before 2 tomorrow, then we can stop at a nearby hostel and resupply and get food. If we miss that window then we have to camp on the side of the river and wait another day to cross. I will be up early but I think I can do it.
