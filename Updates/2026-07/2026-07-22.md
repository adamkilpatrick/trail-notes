Long day. First time I've done 25 miles in a while. Climb up to white cap in the morning was chill but the promised view of katahdin was socked in hard.

![[PXL_20260722_121702682.jpg]]

White cap is considered the "last big climb" but I knew some shit little hill shortly after called "little boardman mountain" was going to be a nightmare and it was.

![[PXL_20260722_143929709.jpg]]

After boardman was some of the 15 flattest miles I've ever experienced. It was nice at first but started to become tedious and boring pretty quickly. I think the mental toll of the hundred mile wilderness is starting to break us. The nobos especially (including myself) feel pretty burned out and ready to close things off. I hope it isn't bringing the mood too down for the flip floppers.

Notyet did his katahdin gift. It was a cool little dynema wallet with a white blaze on it.

Camping tonight right off jo Mary lake. It started dumping rain as soon as we got in so I set up my tarp in big mode so we had a dry place to cook. Once the rain cleared there were some very pretty views.

![[PXL_20260722_221247629.jpg]]
![[PXL_20260723_001024211.MP.jpg]]
![[PXL_20260723_001149885.jpg]]

Tomorrow is 25 more miles to rainbow stream. Flatter than today even. It is our last day of "real" hiking. Next day is a scramble to see if we can camp at the base of katahdin or if Baxter state park is going to fuck us. Day after is Katahdin.
