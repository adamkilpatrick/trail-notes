With a couple days food + 4L water it ended up being about 26 lbs. I ended up getting ~14 miles in which I'm pretty pleased with I guess, especially given that I didn't really start moving until almost 3pm, but I fucked up kinda bad and ended up having to do almost 6 of those miles in the dark.
