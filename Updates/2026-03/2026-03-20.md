Still feeling pretty weak. Still feeling pretty exhausted. Dunno if I'm sick or something or if I am just legitimately becoming worse at this. Maybe just dehydrated.

Committed to Erwin tomorrow tho. It is an actual town with streetlights and whatnot so I booked the nicest hotel that I could and will be taking a zero. Feels bad to be taking one so soon after hot springs but it feels bad to simply exist right now.

Think I can make it to bald mt shelter at least. There's a campsite 3 miles past that I would like to shoot for.

The big ascent of the day was big bald. Decided I'd lock in, put some music on, scarf the rest of my snacks and hit it hard when I got there at 2 pm. Then found this:

![[PXL_20260320_184518676.jpg]]
