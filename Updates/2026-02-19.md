Interesting character I've run into the past few days. Guy wearing a kilt hiking north at about the same pace as us, doesn't say much, his bag is filled with Arby's sandwiches. I would be taking a breather on the trail, he'd appear out of the fog or from behind a rock smoking a cigarette and then walk past me. I'd hit him with a "howdy", he'd grunt in acknowledgement and keep going. Then I wouldn't see him until the exact same interaction played out again. Someone nearby asked if he was thru hiking and his response was "I'm just getting away for a while". Didn't see him today but have thought a lot about that last part.

12.1 miles today was pretty smooth. Rained a lot but I nabbed a bag liner at the resupply so my shit is pretty dry (I also grabbed a ziplock bag of trail mix from the free box because I am in desperate need of Ziploc bags). At low gap shelter now with notyet and bladerunner. We all got here at 2 pm so we've just been chilling and planning out the next week or so. It's about to get real cold. Tomorrow is an easy 7 mile day. Day after is going to be a pretty brutal 15 mile climb. 

Picked up a real nifty adapter for the Sawyer squeeze so the whole system threads together. It feels like a game changer.
