Pretty easy day today. I wanted to check the logbook at the next shelter but mistakenly took a very long side trail for a long time. Wasn't really in a rush today anyway tho so it was fine.

Ran into Van Gogh at Chimney rock. He told me that a critter of some sort got into his ursac last night and ate his leftover pizza slices. My bear can was unperturbed.

![[PXL_20260512_141845487.jpg]]

The rest of the day was very chill, although I was listening to "Into thin air" by Jon Krakauer, which definitely elevated my blood pressure.

I got to quarry gap shelter at around 230, which really tempted me to push past, but this shelter site is by far the nicest I've seen, holy moly.

![[PXL_20260512_184510523.jpg]]
![[PXL_20260512_190530117.jpg]]
![[PXL_20260512_203330486.MP.jpg]]

It even has a dedicated hammock rig (I let some other guy setup there, hunting for good trees is half the fun).
![[PXL_20260512_221146087.jpg]]

Van Gogh and a ton of others showed up here, including one of the guys that helps maintain the shelter. Chatted with them a bit and went to bed early. Tomorrow is 17 miles to the official halfway point where I will then have to eat a half gallon of ice cream so I want to be up early. Not too worried about the miles, but I do fear that the ice cream will be the toughest challenge on the trail so far.
