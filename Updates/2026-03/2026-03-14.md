Short walk to hot springs. Got into town well before I could check in to my cabin so I went to the smokey mtn diner. Woodrow and Tinker were both there. I ate with Woodrow (they gave me wayyy too much food) and he told me some more crazy stories about standing bear. Apparently one of the workers there used to live in a cave on the farm property. He said it was a nice cave tho.
