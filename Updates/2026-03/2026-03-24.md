Slow day. Slept in a bit. It was pretty hot and my lower back is starting to hurt I think from the extra food weight.

Apparently 1 mile south of where I camped last night a wildfire has broken out. I didn't see or smell anything, but the trail was pretty empty today and according to the email I got a bunch of roads are closed. There's a section of detour due to Helene damage, but I didn't take it as Preacher had told me it was fine. It did end up being fine mostly, but there's still some wild destruction.

![[PXL_20260324_174113200.jpg]]
![[PXL_20260324_161039907.jpg]]

Got to Clyde Smith shelter pretty late, expecting to be the only one there, but I could see from the distance that someone had started a fire. Turned out to be Preacher. We chatted a bit, and a guy named Lucky Charm showed up. He's a chill guy. Got his name because he ran across a couple dying bear cubs a few weeks back and called in to get them rescued. He's doing like 25 mile days tho so I probably won't see him again.

Tomorrow is either 12 or 16 miles depending on how I am feeling, then next day is a hostel day before burning into Hampton. Then hopefully a few short days to Damascus.
